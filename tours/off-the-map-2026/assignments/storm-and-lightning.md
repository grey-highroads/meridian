## What we are asking for

We want a storm to come through during Broken Branches. Not weather as decoration behind the band, an actual front that arrives, breaks, and passes, so the room feels different on the other side of it.

The song is about a family tree that has taken damage and is still standing, so the storm has to read as something survived rather than something spectacular. If people walk out talking about the lightning cue we got it wrong. If they walk out quieter than they came in we got it right.

It has to work at a shed, outdoors, at dusk, with real sky behind the stage. It also has to work on the four arena dates where there is no sky at all.

Give us two or three ways to do this before anyone builds anything.
