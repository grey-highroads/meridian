## Direction, version 1

Set by: Nadia Rourke, Creative Director
Set on: 2026-01-14
Stored as given. These are the director's words as they arrived, kept whole and kept apart from anything the brain suggests.

The record is called Off The Map and the show should feel like the title means something. Not a travelogue. The feeling of being somewhere the map stops being useful and you have to read the ground instead.

I want the whole night to sit outdoors. Sheds, dusk, weather coming in over the back of the lawn. Every look should feel like it belongs to a sky rather than to a room. Where we go big I want it to be the sky doing it, not a machine.

Warm and worn as the baseline. Gold, dust, tungsten, the light an hour before dark. Cold only when we earn it, and when we do it should feel like altitude or like weather, never like chrome.

Keep him close. The band plays as a band and the audience is in the room with them. I do not want anything on this tour that puts a wall of technology between Dierks and the people in the lawn.

The bluegrass thread is not a segment. It runs under the whole show. Whatever we build has to hold an acoustic circle at centre stage without looking like the production stopped and something else started.

Three things I do not want. Anything that reads as arena rock. Anything glossy. Anything that would make him look like he takes himself too seriously.
