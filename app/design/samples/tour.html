Tour Direction

Build the show around scale, restraint, and a clear sense of forward motion. The screen world should feel architectural rather than decorative, with each visual change tied to a musical or physical event on stage.

Keep the artist readable at every venue. Use negative space deliberately, avoid constant surface movement, and let the largest moments arrive through contrast rather than visual density.

Across the set, the material should feel connected without becoming one repeated treatment. Preserve room for lighting, camera, and performance to lead.
