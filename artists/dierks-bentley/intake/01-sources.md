## Excluded, and why

Recorded so the script inherits the exclusion rules rather than rediscovering them.

- Ticket resellers and aggregator SEO pages. Seen and rejected: seatgeek.com, concertfix.com, dierksbentleyconcerts.com, dierksbentleytour2027.us. The last of these carries an invented album title and a fabricated tour, which is a clean example of why the tier list exists.
- Forums, Reddit, and comment sections, excluded by the ruling.
- Fan sites and fan wikis.
- Private and paywalled community groups.

## One judgment call for the operator

Wikipedia is not in the ruled tier list and I have not counted it as a source. Its tour and discography pages carry per tour date lists, chronology, and personnel that no single tier 1 to tier 6 source assembles in one place, and its own citations point at primary sources.

Recommendation: treat Wikipedia as an index rather than a source. Read it to find primary sources and never write a claim whose only evidence is a Wikipedia sentence. That keeps the ruling intact and still gets the chronology. If you want it admitted as a source in its own right, that is a change to the tier list and belongs in the thesis.

Pages I would use as an index if that is ruled acceptable: the artist page, the discography page, the Hot Country Knights page, and the per tour pages for Riser Tour, Somewhere on a Beach Tour, and the rest of the chronology.

## What this list does not yet cover

Named as gaps now so step three does not quietly skip them.

- Album art direction, photography, and music video direction credits by era. No tier 1 to tier 4 source found yet names an art director or a video director for any Bentley era. Visual language is a ruled facet and this is the thinnest part of the list.
- Tours before 2016. Production and lighting trade coverage thins out fast going backward. The trade tier is strong from Somewhere on a Beach forward and weak before it.
- Wardrobe and styling credits across all eras.
- What the brand avoids. No source on this list exists to answer that facet directly. It has to be read out of the other five, and it is the facet most likely to come back thin.
