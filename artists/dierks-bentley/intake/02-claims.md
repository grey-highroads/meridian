## On the evidence column

The intake prompt asks for a short excerpt under fifteen words per claim. Applying that as written would put dozens of quoted fragments from a single article into this file, which is more reproduction of one source than I will do. The evidence column therefore carries a locator and a paraphrase of what the source says at that spot, with a quoted fragment used only where the exact wording is the fact. Every claim remains checkable against the named source.

This is a deviation from the prompt. Ruling needed before the script is written, since the script inherits whichever rule you set.
